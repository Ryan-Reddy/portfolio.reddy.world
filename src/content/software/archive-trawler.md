Archive Trawler searches many archives at once by name, so you don't have to go through each archive on its own. I made it as my first-year IPASS project at Hogeschool Utrecht, in Java. It runs at [archivetrawler.reddy.world](https://archivetrawler.reddy.world). The first visit after a quiet spell takes up to a minute while the server wakes up.

## What you can do

- Search by surname, name prefix, first name and keywords, between two years.
- Choose which archives to search, and search them all in one go.
- Search more than 200 Dutch archives through OpenArchieven.nl, among them Stadsarchief Amsterdam, Nationaal Archief, Gelders Archief and AlleFriezen, and filter by place, period, kind of record and role.
- Also search the UK National Archives (Discovery) and Europeana's photos and objects.

## How it works

**Many archives, one question.** You fill in the form once and tick the archives you want. Archive Trawler searches them at the same time, by name, so you don't have to visit each one.

**A Dutch name in three parts.** The form asks for the prefix, such as "van" or "de", in its own field, next to the surname and the first name.
