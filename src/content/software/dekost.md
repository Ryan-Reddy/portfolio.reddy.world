What does a gram of protein actually cost? Dekost answers that for the food in Dutch supermarkets. It ranks foods by protein per euro, and lets you filter and compare them in a table and on a chart. The nutrition data comes from the Dutch RIVM NEVO database (version 2025/9.0) and from USDA FoodData Central.

Dekost is in beta, and you see it after signing in: with Google, Facebook, Microsoft or an email address. That's why this page shows a single picture, taken by me inside the app.

## What you can do

- Filter by kind of food: meat, fish, eggs and dairy, legumes, meat substitutes, seeds, nuts, vegetables, grains, mushrooms and fruit.
- Plot the foods on a chart, by protein density against the price of a gram of protein.
- Pick a food to see its nutrition facts, how its calories split between carbohydrate, fat and protein, how filling it is, and where to buy it for what price.
- See where a food ranks among all the others.
- Sign in to keep your profile and your food diaries in step across devices.

## The hard parts

**Price meets nutrition.** Dekost puts a supermarket price next to the nutrition data of each food, so the cost of a gram of protein can be compared across all of them.

**Two databases, credited.** The nutrition data comes from the Dutch NEVO tables of the RIVM and from USDA FoodData Central, and both are credited at the foot of the page.
